Gohancho Support

A meal planner that suggests a complete home-cooked dinner from your country's everyday dishes.

Frequently asked questions

Will it keep suggesting the same dishes?

When you save a plan or tap "Made today", dishes you made within the set number of days (10 by default) are left out, and the App avoids repeating yesterday's meat or fish. You can change the number of days in Settings (the gear icon).

How do I change my country or language?

Open Settings and choose your country or region. The screen language follows your country; you can also choose a language in Settings.

Can I see recipes from other countries?

Yes. Tap "World recipes" at the bottom and pick a country. Your daily meal plans stay based on your own country.

How do I send the plan to my family?

Tap the send button under today's meal or the weekly plan. The App opens the messaging app most used in your country (or the share sheet) so you can pick who to send it to.

I have disliked foods or allergies

Dishes using ingredients you choose under "Ingredients to avoid" in Settings are never suggested. If you have allergies, always check the ingredients of seasonings and processed foods yourself.

How does the free trial work?

Start the monthly plan and your first month is free. When the free month ends, your Apple Account is charged the monthly price and it renews each month automatically, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. There is no yearly commitment; cancel anytime. The free trial is available to new subscribers only.

How do I cancel Gohancho Plus?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Gohancho, and tap Cancel Subscription.

I got a new phone. What about my purchase?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap "Restore purchases" in Settings. You can move your meal history with "Move or back up your data" in Settings.

About the dish illustrations

Each dish has an original illustration built into the App, so it shows even offline. You can also replace it with your own photo (stored only on your device).
