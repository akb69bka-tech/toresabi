Privacy Policy

Gohancho (the "App") · Effective date: [enter publication date]

1. Information that never leaves your device

Your meal history, favorites, fridge ingredients, pantry items, disliked ingredients, settings such as household size and country, recipes you add, and photos you take of your dishes are stored only on your device. They are never sent to the developer's servers, and the developer cannot see them.

3. Advertising and analytics

The App shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking tools.

4. Camera and photos

The App uses the camera or photo library only when you tap "Add a photo of yours". The photo you choose is resized and stored on your device only. It is never uploaded.

5. Deleting your data

Use "Erase all data" in Settings to delete your history and settings from the device. Deleting the App also deletes all of its data on the device.

6. Your rights (EU/UK GDPR and similar laws)

Because the developer does not collect or store your personal data, there is no personal data held by the developer to access, correct or delete. For purchase data, please contact Apple. For any questions, contact us below.
